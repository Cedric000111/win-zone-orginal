WIN ZONE privacy policy — frontend draft.

This is a frontend product draft. The final privacy policy must be reviewed before launch to ensure compliance with applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the final platform configuration, WIN ZONE may process account information, contact information, country information, transaction records, match records, support requests and security information.

2. Account Information

Information supplied during registration may be used to create and secure the user's account and provide platform services.

3. Wallet and Transaction Information

Deposits, withdrawals, match stakes and payouts may require transaction records for account management, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and applicable legal requirements.

4. Security

Production WIN ZONE systems should use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect account and transaction information.

5. Payment Providers

Payment providers may process information required to complete payments according to their own applicable policies and terms.

6. Notifications

Browser notifications require permission from the user's browser and device.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The production platform may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, preferences and platform functionality.

8. Data Retention

Production retention periods should be established based on operational, legal, security and regulatory requirements.

9. User Rights

Applicable privacy laws may provide users with rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or other handling of personal information.
